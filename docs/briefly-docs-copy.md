## The problem

### Most teams don't struggle because information is missing. They struggle because it is buried.

Policies, SOPs, contracts, circulars, and approvals exist across folders and systems. But finding the right answer still takes too long — and when it is found, teams may not be sure it is the latest version or something they can confidently act on.

Briefly Docs is built to reduce that friction.

## Search across documents

### Find what matters — across your entire library

No more manual digging through folders, drives, or email threads. Ask in plain language and get to the right document — and the right passage — instantly.

- Search across PDFs, Word files, and folders at once
- Natural language — no query syntax to learn
- Results ranked by relevance, not last modified

## Answers with proof

### Every answer tied back to its exact source

Every answer is linked to the exact paragraph it came from, with document name, page number, and section reference — so your team can verify before acting, not just trust the output.

- Source paragraph highlighted in the original document
- Document name, page, and section cited on every answer
- Acts as evidence, not just information

## Version awareness

### Always know which version the answer came from

Stop acting on outdated policies or superseded procedures. Briefly Docs tells you exactly which document version an answer references — so version confusion becomes a problem of the past.

- Document version cited on every answer
- Flags when a newer version of a file exists
- Historical versions still queryable, but clearly marked

## Workflow support

### Not just search — built into how work gets done

Briefly Docs is built around document-heavy workflows, not just passive search. Handle queries that feed directly into approvals, actions, and decisions without switching tools.

- Link answers directly to approval or routing steps
- Handle compliance and operational queries in context
- Share verified answers with teams — not raw files

## How it works

### From question to verified answer in seconds

#### 01. Upload your documents

Connect or upload your document library — PDFs, Word files, folders, or integrated sources.

#### 02. Ask in plain language

Type a question the way your team would ask it. No query syntax, no filters to learn.

#### 03. Get an answer with its source

See the answer alongside the exact excerpt it came from, with document name and version.

#### 04. Act with confidence

Share, verify, or act on the answer knowing exactly where it came from and when it was last updated.
